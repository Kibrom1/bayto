## Shared rules
- Colours only from index.css tokens (--ok-soft, --warn-soft, --danger-soft ...), no raw hex in components. Neutral default, green success, amber limits/pause/failure, red danger. Primary and caution never share a hue.
- Breakpoint 640px for mobile layout; verify at 390 and 1280.
- Targets >=44px mobile, >=24px desktop. Every control has an accessible name; aria-label starts with visible text; one <h1> per page; status changes use aria-live.
- Toasts through the room's flash helper; no new inline status text. No disabled form as a primary slot; disabled controls say why. Plain copy, no env var names.
- Edit only your own files; if you need a change in someone else's file, note it in your final report instead. Keep `npx tsc -b` and `npx vitest run` clean.
- Update docs/decisions.md with one short entry for your workstream (append at the end; merge conflicts there are expected and resolved by keeping both).
