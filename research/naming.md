# Research: product name

2026-09-25. Working title was "The Assembly". **Chosen: Bayto (ባይቶ).**

## Tigrigna candidates (chosen language)

| Name | Meaning | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bayto (ባይቶ)** | Traditional village assembly: the community gathers, debates openly, decides together | Chosen. Short, easy to say in English, fits the Sened/Gashana family. Also the name of Eritrea's regional assemblies (slight political association) |
| Zete (ዘተ) | Discussion, dialogue | Fits chat/collaboration; very easy to say |
| Kiti'i (ክትዕ) | Debate, argument | Direct match for debate; the ʿ sound is hard for non-speakers |
| Wa'ela (ዋዕላ) | Conference, congress | Formal; suits the platform idea |
| Mikhri (ምኽሪ) | Advice, counsel | Decision-support positioning |
| Wefera (ወፈራ) | Collective work campaign | Suits collaboration |

## Earlier rounds

- **Amharic:** Shengo (ሸንጎ, traditional council of elders — first recommendation), Gubae (ጉባኤ, conference/assembly), Mikir (ምክር, counsel), Debo (ደቦ, communal work).
- **English:** Quorum, Conclave, Parley, Agora, Moot.

Domain and trademark availability were not checked.
